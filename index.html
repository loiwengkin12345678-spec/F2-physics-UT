Form 3 Physics: Astrophysics, Wave Basics, Calculations & Practice Assessment

Astrophysics Core Notes

  • Solar System Location: Located in the Milky Way galaxy[cite: 3, 9].
  • Planetary Orbit Force: Kept in orbit around the Sun by gravity (gravitational attraction)[cite: 3, 9].
  • Galaxies: A large collection of stars is called a galaxy[cite: 3, 9].
  • Stellar Life Cycles: Medium stars become Red Giants $\rightarrow$ White Dwarfs[cite: 3, 6, 9]. Massive stars become Red Supergiants $\rightarrow$ Supernova $\rightarrow$ Neutron Star or Black Hole[cite: 3, 6, 9].
  • Red-Shift: Wavelength of light from distant galaxies moving away from us increases (red-shifted)[cite: 3, 9].
  • Big Bang Theory: Supported by Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB) radiation[cite: 3, 9].

Wave Basics Notes

  • Transverse Waves: Particles vibrate perpendicular to the wave direction (e.g., light waves).
  • Longitudinal Waves: Particles vibrate parallel to the wave direction featuring compressions and rarefactions (e.g., sound waves)[cite: 4].
  • Amplitude: Maximum displacement of a wave from its rest position[cite: 4].
  • Wavelength ($\lambda$): Distance between identical points on adjacent waves[cite: 4].
  • Frequency ($f$): Number of oscillations per second, measured in Hertz (Hz)[cite: 4, 8].

Key Formulas & Calculations

1. Wave Speed Equation

v = f × λ
(Wave Speed = Frequency × Wavelength)[cite: 4, 8]

2. Frequency & Time Period

f = number of oscillations / time[cite: 10]
Time Period (T) = 1 / f[cite: 4, 8]

3. Orbital Speed Equation

v = (2 π r) / T
(Orbital Speed = 2 × π × Orbital Radius / Time Period)[cite: 5]

Form 3 Physics Practice Assessment

Test your knowledge with these Fill-in-the-Blanks, Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ), and Short/Long Answer Questions.

Section A: Fill in the Blanks

1. Our solar system is located in the ________ galaxy.[cite: 3, 9]
Answer: Milky Way[cite: 3, 9]
2. The force that keeps planets in orbit around the Sun is ________.[cite: 3, 9]
Answer: gravity (or gravitational attraction)[cite: 3, 9]
3. The equation linking wave speed, frequency, and wavelength is v = ________ × λ.[cite: 4, 8]
Answer: f (frequency)[cite: 4, 8]

Section B: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)

1. What is made of ice and dust, orbits the Sun in a highly elliptical orbit, and travels faster when nearer the Sun?[cite: 3, 9]
  • A. An asteroid[cite: 9]
  • B. A comet[cite: 9]
  • C. A moon[cite: 9]
  • D. A planet[cite: 9]
Answer: B. A comet[cite: 3, 9]
2. Light from distant galaxies observed with a longer wavelength than expected is called:[cite: 9]
  • A. Blue-shift[cite: 9]
  • B. Refraction[cite: 9]
  • C. Reflection[cite: 9]
  • D. Red-shift[cite: 9]
Answer: D. Red-shift[cite: 9]
3. Which of these stars has the shortest main sequence lifetime?[cite: 3]
  • A. A small red star[cite: 3]
  • B. A medium yellow star[cite: 3]
  • C. A large blue star[cite: 3]
  • D. They all have the same lifetime[cite: 3]
Answer: C. A large blue star[cite: 3] (Massive stars burn through their hydrogen fuel much faster).

Section C: Short & Long Answer Questions

1. Describe the life cycle of a massive star starting from a cloud of dust and gas up to its final stages.[cite: 3]
Marking Scheme:
1. Starts as a nebula (cloud of dust and gas) which collapses due to gravity to form a protostar[cite: 3].
2. Nuclear fusion begins, turning it into a main sequence star[cite: 3].
3. When hydrogen runs out, it expands into a red supergiant[cite: 3].
4. The core collapses rapidly in a massive supernova explosion[cite: 3].
5. The remaining core forms a very dense neutron star, or a black hole if the mass is extremely high[cite: 3].
2. Calculation: A signal generator is set to 50 Hz, and the measured wavelength is 6.8 m. Calculate the speed of sound in air.[cite: 8]
Working & Answer:
Formula: v = f × λ[cite: 4, 8]
v = 50 Hz × 6.8 m[cite: 8]
v = 340 m/s[cite: 8]
